# Kombeweeg

Kombeweeg turns the activities friends already record elsewhere into shared, fair competition between people and between the groups they belong to.

## Groups and time

**Guild**:
A lasting group of people built around one or more focus sports. A person can belong to any number of guilds.
_Avoid_: Campaign, club, team, crew

**Focus sport**:
A sport a guild is built around. An activity can only count toward a guild whose focus sports include it.
_Avoid_: Main sport, discipline, category

**Founder**:
The person who created a guild. Appoints its officers.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin

**Officer**:
A guild member appointed by the founder who can set guild milestones, issue and accept challenges, and join campaigns for the guild.
_Avoid_: Moderator, captain, admin

**Year**:
One annual cycle in a guild's life, starting on the guild's creation date or the first day of a chosen weather season.
_Avoid_: Season, campaign period

**Check-in**:
One quarter of a guild's year, labelled by weather season (Summer, Autumn, Winter, Spring) when the year starts on one; the point where a guild sees whether it is on pace for its milestones. A check-in has no targets of its own.
_Avoid_: Quarter, review, sprint

**Milestone**:
A person's own target for a whole guild year.
_Avoid_: Yearly goal, objective

**Challenge**:
A contest between guilds over a fixed period that is set by the challenge and has nothing to do with any guild's check-ins.
_Avoid_: Match, battle, versus

**Roster**:
The members of a guild who opted in to a challenge before it started. Only they count toward the guild's challenge score.
_Avoid_: Lineup, squad, participants

**Quest**:
A target that a whole guild works toward together over a period an officer sets. Nobody is ranked; it is only for motivation.
_Avoid_: Internal challenge, group goal, shared milestone

**Campaign**:
A cause-backed goal (for example a charity drive) that guilds join and people opt into. Every activity of an opted-in person counts. It is about coming together, not competing.
_Avoid_: Fundraiser, drive, quest

## Activities

**Activity source**:
An outside app that supplies a person's activities, such as Strava.
_Avoid_: Integration, provider, connection

**Allocation**:
The one guild whose challenges an activity counts toward. Allocation affects nothing else: every guild a person belongs to still sees their activities, with ones allocated elsewhere greyed out.
_Avoid_: Split, assignment, routing

## Fairness

**Baseline**:
What a person normally does over a period of a given length, worked out from their own history. Nobody can set it by hand.
_Avoid_: Normal, average, usual

**Ambition**:
How far up a person's own history a target sits, so a target equal to their best month of the year is equally ambitious for anyone.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, stretch, increase

**Sidelined**:
A period in which a person has declared they can't compete. No reason is recorded.
_Avoid_: Injured, ill, sick leave, inactive
