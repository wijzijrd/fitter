# Ambition and score are percentiles of a person's own history

Guild members train different sports at very different volumes, and the group wants a fair comparison. So both a target's ambition and a person's score are measured as percentiles of that person's own past periods of the same length: "this would be your best month of the year" means the same for a 3-workouts-a-week lifter as for a 250 km-a-month runner. Rank comes from the achievement percentile, not from progress toward the target, so setting a low target gains nothing. The baseline is computed from synced history and can't be edited, and sidelined days are left out of it.

## Considered Options

- **Percentage increase over a baseline** (3 → 6 workouts = +100%, 250 → 300 km = +20%). Rejected because it favours people with low baselines, even though small gains near someone's ceiling are often the harder effort.
- **Declared baseline.** Rejected because it invites sandbagging.
- **Ambition × completion.** Rejected because it rewards setting huge targets with no intention of hitting them.

## Consequences

- Anything that gets ranked or scored (milestones, challenge metrics) uses exactly one metric. Goals with several clauses are kept for private personal goals only.
- A person needs enough history before their score can be trusted, so sign-in pulls about two years of history. Until there's enough, the person is marked provisional and left out of ranking.
