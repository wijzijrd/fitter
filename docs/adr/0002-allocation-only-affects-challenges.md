# Allocation only affects challenges

A person can belong to several guilds, and each of their activities is allocated to at most one guild whose focus sports include it. That allocation is used only to score challenges between guilds, where counting the same activity twice would distort the result. Everything else ignores it: every guild a person belongs to sees all of their activities (ones allocated elsewhere are greyed out), and milestones, quests, campaigns and personal goals count every matching activity. An activity's allocation can change until it is 48 hours old or the challenge it counts toward ends, whichever comes first, so nobody can move activities between guilds to swing a result at the last minute.

## Considered Options

- **Allocation decides visibility and every count.** Rejected because it hides a friend's work from guilds they also belong to and makes a person's own milestones depend on routing choices that only matter for competition.
- **No allocation, every activity counts everywhere.** Rejected because a person in several guilds would count the same work in each, unfairly boosting those guilds in challenges.
